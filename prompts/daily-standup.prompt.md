# Daily Standup Helper

Pull today's work picture from Jira and help me prepare for standup.

## What to look up

1. **My open tickets** — everything assigned to me that isn't Done or Closed
2. **Tickets I updated yesterday** — what did I actually work on?
3. **Any tickets I'm blocked on** — anything with a "Blocked" label or status
4. **Tickets due soon** — anything due in the next 3 days

## How to present it

Format the summary like a standup, with three sections:

**Yesterday** — What I worked on (based on recent updates)

**Today** — What I plan to work on (highest priority open tickets)

**Blockers** — Anything stopping me, and a suggested next step for each

---

Keep it short — one or two bullet points per section.  
If you find something that looks urgent or overdue, flag it at the top.

After the summary, ask me:  
*"Is there anything you'd like to update or flag before standup?"*
