# Sprint Planning Helper

Let's set up your next sprint. I'll help you review the backlog, understand capacity, and build a realistic sprint plan.

## Step 1 — Current situation

First, let me check what's going on:
- What's in the backlog (unstarted tickets)
- What carried over from the last sprint
- What's marked as high priority

Which project should I look at? (Give me the project key or name)

---

## Step 2 — Capacity

A few quick questions to plan realistically:

- How many people are working this sprint?
- How long is the sprint? (e.g., 1 week, 2 weeks)
- Is anyone on holiday or unavailable?
- Are there any fixed deadlines or deliverables coming up?

---

## Step 3 — What I'll help you build

Once I have this info, I'll:

1. **Suggest a sprint goal** — a short sentence that describes what the team is achieving
2. **Recommend which tickets to include** — based on priority, size, and dependencies
3. **Flag risks** — tickets that are too vague, too big, or depend on something unfinished
4. **Draft a planning summary** — a message you can share with your team

---

## After we plan

I can also:
- Create a "Sprint Planning" Jira ticket as a reference document
- Move selected tickets into the sprint for you (after confirmation)
- Write a message to your team announcing the sprint
