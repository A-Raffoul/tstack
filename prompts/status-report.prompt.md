# Status Report Generator

I'll pull the latest from Jira and write a status report you can share with your team or stakeholders.

## Tell me what you need

Answer one or both of these:

1. **Which project or sprint?** — Give me the project name/key, or say "current sprint"
2. **Who is this report for?** — e.g., "my manager", "the client", "the whole team", "a board meeting"

---

## What the report will include

**Summary** — A one-paragraph overview of where things stand

**Completed this sprint/period** — Tickets that were closed or moved to Done

**In Progress** — What's actively being worked on right now

**Coming Up Next** — What's planned for the next sprint or period

**Risks & Blockers** — Anything that could delay the project, with suggested actions

**Overall Status** — Green / Yellow / Red with a one-line reason

---

## Tone options

Tell me if you want the report to be:
- **Formal** — for clients, senior stakeholders, board-level
- **Casual** — for internal team updates, Slack messages
- **Mixed** — formal summary up top, casual notes below

If you don't specify, I'll write it in a professional but readable tone.
